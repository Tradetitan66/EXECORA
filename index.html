FAQs

Questions, answered.

I don’t have a website yet - is that okay?
Absolutely. Many of the businesses we support are starting their online presence from scratch. We take it from there, step by step, at your pace.
What exactly do I get for £5?
A personalised homepage prototype designed around your business - a mockup you can look at, share and keep. It shows exactly how your website could look before you commit to a full site. There’s no obligation to continue.
How long does it take to get started?
After a quick chat about your business, we move quickly - most people see the foundations of their new presence before they know it. Your £5 prototype lands within 24 hours.
Do I need any technical knowledge?
None at all. We handle the technical side so you can keep doing what you do best - running your business.
Do you work with businesses outside Edinburgh?
Execora starts in Edinburgh, with a vision to grow globally. Even if you’re nearby but just over the border, we’d love to hear from you.
Do I need to buy my own domain name?
Yes. Domain name purchase is not included in any Execora plan and must be purchased separately by the client. Execora will assist with connecting and configuring your domain for your website. Domain ownership stays with you.
