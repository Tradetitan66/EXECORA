£39 plan

Execora £39 Monthly Plan Terms

Price and billing

The plan costs £39 per month, charged to your chosen payment method when you subscribe and monthly thereafter. The minimum commitment is 12 months, with 12 monthly payments totalling £468. Any separately agreed setup fee or additional work will be shown to you before you agree to pay for it.

Initial term and renewal

Your initial term begins when you subscribe and lasts 12 months. After that, the plan continues on a rolling monthly basis at £39 per month until cancelled under the process below. We will tell you in advance about any proposed price change. The agreed price will not change during your initial 12-month term without your agreement.

Cancellation

You may give notice during the initial term so that your plan ends when the 12 months are complete. To cancel, send a WhatsApp message to +44 7345 384868 with your name and the email address you used to subscribe. We will acknowledge your request and confirm your final service and billing dates.

After the initial term, you may cancel by giving at least 30 calendar days' notice by WhatsApp. Your plan will end no earlier than the end of the initial 12 months and 30 calendar days after we receive your notice. We will not charge for a new monthly billing period that begins after your confirmed end date.

Cancelling a payment method or a payment through your bank does not, by itself, give us notice to end the plan. Please message us so we can confirm the end date and stop future subscription charges.

Your legal rights

Nothing in these terms limits any cancellation, refund, or other rights you have under applicable law. If you are buying as a consumer and believe you have a statutory right to cancel, contact us on WhatsApp at +44 7345 384868. We will explain any amount payable for services you expressly asked us to start during a statutory cancellation period.
